# FinPulse Evaluation & Methodology Report

> **Dataset Disclosure:** All customer trajectories in FinPulse (1,000 customers × 730 days = 730,000 rows across 8 segments) are **100% synthetic**, deterministically generated by `src/customer_generator.py` (`seed=42`). Results validate internal consistency, leakage behavior, baseline comparisons, and runtime trade-offs — not real banking outcomes.

## 2. Risk Classifier Audit (Leakage, Rebuilt Split & Forward Prediction)

### 2.1 Label & Feature Definition
- **Target Label (`risk_tier`):** Bucketed mean of rolling 180-day `compute_fhs()` scores (30-day step) across each customer's 730-day series: `High (<60)`, `Medium (60–74.99)`, `Low (>=75)`. *(Audit fix: corrected a stale docstring in `ml_pipeline.py` that misstated the thresholds as `[35, 60]`.)*
- **FHS Formula (`src/fhs_calculator.py`):**
  $$\text{FHS} = 0.4 \cdot \text{balance\_trend} + 0.3 \cdot \text{income\_regularity} + 0.2 \cdot \text{spending\_volatility} + 0.1 \cdot \text{debt\_ratio}$$

| Feature | Definition (730d window) | Relation to Target (`risk_tier`) | Spearman ρ vs `_fhs_mean` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bal_trend` | OLS daily balance slope | **Direct input** to `balance_trend` (40% of FHS) | `0.9601` |
| `income_regularity` | CV of monthly balance deltas | **Direct input** to `income_regularity` (30% of FHS) | `-0.9529` |
| `bal_mean` | Mean daily balance | **Direct denominator** in `spending_volatility` (20% of FHS) + segment scale | `0.9426` |
| `vel_mean` | Mean absolute daily balance delta | Proxies `std(daily_changes)` in `spending_volatility` (20% of FHS) | `0.8756` |
| `pct_negative_days` | Fraction of days `balance < 0` | **Direct input** to `debt_ratio` (10% of FHS) | `-0.0613` |
| `fhs_std` | Std dev of rolling 180d FHS values | **Target-derived** from the exact array whose mean defines `risk_tier` | `-0.7050` |
| `bal_std` | Std dev of daily balance | Same-window dispersion + segment scale | `0.9593` |
| `bal_cv` | `bal_std / |bal_mean|` | Same-window relative dispersion | `0.5664` |
| `vel_max` | Max single-day absolute change | Non-formula feature (still scales with synthetic segment salary/rent) | `0.8275` |
| `max_drawdown` | Max peak-to-trough balance decline | Non-formula feature (reflects monthly rent outflow size) | `0.6279` |
| `runway_mean` | `bal_mean / avg daily outflow` (clipped 365d) | Non-formula ratio (strongly coupled to `bal_mean` & segment scale) | `0.9539` |

### 2.2 Leakage Proof (Original Same-Window Setup)
Evaluating the original 11 features on the same 730-day window (80/20 stratified customer split):

| Model / Baseline | Accuracy (95% CI) | Macro F1 (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Majority Class | `52.5%` `[45.5%, 59.5%]` | `0.230` `[0.209, 0.249]` |
| Exact FHS Formula Threshold Rule | `96.0%` `[93.5%, 98.5%]` | `0.959` `[0.928, 0.984]` |
| Logistic Regression | `97.5%` `[95.0%, 99.5%]` | `0.974` `[0.949, 0.995]` |
| Random Forest | `95.5%` `[92.5%, 98.0%]` | `0.952` `[0.920, 0.981]` |

**Plain Verdict:** *The original classifier reproduces a deterministic threshold rule (`bucket(FHS, [60, 75])`) from its own formula components computed on the exact same 730-day time window.*

### 2.3 Rebuilt Evaluation (Customer-Grouped, Time-Aware Year 1 → Year 2, No Formula Features)
- **Split:** Features extracted strictly from **Year 1** (`days 0..364`) predicting **Year 2** (`days 365..729`) `risk_tier` on disjoint 80/20 customers (plus 5-fold customer-grouped CV).
- **Features Kept (non-formula):** `vel_max`, `max_drawdown`, `runway_mean` (all 10 direct FHS components, `fhs_std`, and `bal_*` / `vel_mean` / `pct_negative_days` / `income_regularity` excluded).

| Model / Baseline | Test Accuracy (95% CI) | Test Macro F1 (95% CI) | 5-Fold CV Acc / Macro-F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Majority Class (`Low`) | `58.5%` `[52.0%, 65.5%]` | `0.246` `[0.228, 0.264]` | — |
| Year-1 FHS Threshold Rule | `66.0%` `[59.5%, 72.5%]` | `0.645` `[0.577, 0.709]` | — |
| Logistic Regression | `76.0%` `[70.0%, 81.5%]` | `0.688` `[0.622, 0.748]` | `77.2%` (±2.4%) / `0.672` |
| Random Forest | `76.5%` `[70.5%, 82.5%]` | `0.709` `[0.639, 0.777]` | `78.1%` (±2.8%) / `0.707` |

**Interpretation:** Even after temporal separation and dropping direct FHS formula inputs, `vel_max`, `max_drawdown`, and `runway_mean` still identify each customer's synthetic segment (`eta^2 = 0.9566` of FHS variance is explained by segment design parameters). Year-1 FHS Threshold Rule drops to `66.0%` because growing balances lower `spending_volatility` over time, shifting borderline customers upward in Year 2.

**Honest Interpretation:** Because `customer_generator.py` generates i.i.d. daily expense shocks (`p = 0.0027/day`), there is no latent deteriorating trajectory in the synthetic generator. On strictly disjoint 180-day windows, a simple mean-reversion rule (`fhs_now - train_segment_mean`) outperforms both Logistic Regression and Random Forest (`AUC 0.806 vs 0.722`). On the 30-day rolling horizon (`AUC 0.846`), ML captures segment volatility dispersion plus rolling 180-day window boundary roll-off (`[t-180, t-150)` exiting the window).

### 3.3 Where Live Seasonal Holt-Winters (`180d`, `m=30`) Wins and Loses
- **Where it wins (`8/8` vs Naive at `h=1..30`, `+20.3%` MAE; `6/8` vs 30d Seasonal-Naive):** Captures the monthly day-1 salary / day-2 rent cycle across all segments, replacing the legacy 45-day unseasonal Holt model (which lost `0/8` vs Naive at `-31.3%` MAE).
- **Where it loses at 30d vs 30d Seasonal-Naive (`2/8` segments):** On `Retirees` (`0.700` vs `0.640`) and `Young Professionals` (`0.798` vs `0.768`), restricting the live model to a 180-day window gives slightly higher 30d MAE than repeating the last 30 days (full expanding history wins `8/8` over Seasonal-Naive).
- **Where it loses at short horizons (`h=1..7`, `4/8` segments vs Naive):** Over days 1–7, Naive persistence achieves lower MAE on **`IT Salaried`** (`0.241` vs `0.292`), **`Govt/PSU`** (`0.190` vs `0.238`), **`Retirees`** (`0.503` vs `0.528`), and **`Young Professionals`** (`0.573` vs `0.591`) because short-term FHS movements are dominated by random daily noise rather than the monthly cycle.

## 4. Customer-Sampling Speedup & Accuracy Impact (`180d` Production Window)

Measured via `python src/benchmark.py` (`30` sampled vs `125` full customers per segment, deterministic `zlib.crc32` selection):

| Workload | Scalar 125c | Scalar 30c | Scalar Sampling Speedup | Vectorized 30c (125c) | Accuracy Impact (`30c` vs `125c`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heatmap (`compute_segment_fhs`) | `2.994s` | `0.947s` | **`3.16×`** | `0.278s` (`0.284s`) | Mean FHS MAE **`0.33`** pts, Max **`0.79`** pts (`Daily Wage`), Risk-tier agreement **`8/8`** |
| Forecaster (`forecast_all_segments`, `180d`, `m=30`) | `676.59s` | `87.70s` | **`7.71×`** | `4.983s` (`5.747s`) | Hist FHS MAE **`0.56`** pts, Forecast MAE **`0.65`** pts (Max **`1.41`** on `Gig/Freelance`), Alert agreement **`1/1`** (`Daily Wage`) |

**Key Engineering Finding:** On the `180d` production lookback, sampling `30` vs `125` customers yields a real **`7.71×`** scalar speedup on forecasting (`676.59s -> 87.70s`) and **`3.16×`** on heatmap (`2.99s -> 0.95s`). Vectorizing `compute_fhs_matrix()` across customer columns gives a further **`17.60×`** speedup (`87.70s -> 4.98s` for `30c`; `5.75s` for all `125c`) with `0.00` numerical parity diff, so once vectorized, sampling only saves `1.15×` (`0.76s`).

## 5. Statistical Tests & Clustering (Synthetic Sanity Checks)

- **One-way ANOVA (`_fhs_mean` across 8 segments):** $F = 3121.70$, $p \approx 0$, $\eta^2 = 0.9566$ (segment parameters account for `95.7%` of FHS variance by simulation design).
- **Spearman Rank Correlation (`runway_mean` vs `_fhs_mean`):** $\rho = 0.9539$, $p \approx 0$. Note: while `runway_mean` is not one of the 4 weighted FHS terms, both increase monotonically with segment balance scale in the synthetic generator.
- **K-Means Clustering (`k = 2..10`):** Peak silhouette `0.519` at `k = 2` (roughly separating high-drift salaried segments from volatile/lower-margin segments); 2D PCA explains `74.4%` variance (`64.2%` + `10.2%`).
